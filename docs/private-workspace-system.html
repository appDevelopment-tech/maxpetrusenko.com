Decision matrix

The solo-first requirement matters. The right system is the one that gets you a clean operator shell now without poisoning future auth, bookings, or payment boundaries.

Option Speed now Later scale Main problem
Reuse TellMe Medium Low Shared auth, shared redirect URLs, mixed product concerns
Custom everything Low High Too much calendar and billing logic before demand is proven
Dedicated Supabase + Cal.com + Stripe High High One extra vendor to manage, but clean boundaries

Why this wins

Identity stays boring

Supabase Auth with Google sign-in. Cookie SSR. RLS-backed membership. No homegrown auth surface.

Scheduling stays specialized

Cal.com owns availability, slots, calendars, teams, routing, reminders, and future operator delegation.

Payments stay compliant

Stripe Checkout handles deposits, packages, subscriptions, and later customer portal flows.

Signal chain

Auth
Max signs in with Google. Supabase callback exchanges code for a cookie session and membership is checked with RLS.
CRM
Workspace loads people, teams, notes, and current status from Supabase.
Book
Visitor books via Cal.com. Availability, event type rules, and calendar conflicts are handled there.
Pay
Payment runs through Stripe Checkout. Webhooks sync final state into the workspace record.
Ops
Later, assistants or practitioners get role-based slices of the same system rather than a second admin app.

Red flags

Risk
Do not make TellMe the auth or data home for this workspace unless you intentionally want one shared operator platform across products.
Risk
Do not custom-build booking before you know Cal.com blocks a real requirement.
Risk
Do not split access control between an env allowlist and database membership long term.
